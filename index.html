About Me

I'm a Junior in Electrical Engineering, minoring in Robotics, at North Dakota State University — a National Merit Commended Scholar and Dean's List honoree carrying a 4.0 GPA. My path started in software: two years of internship experience spanning full-stack web development and enterprise IT. It's since grown into embedded systems and hardware — I led the electrical and firmware design of TerraCleaner, an autonomous litter-collection robot that won four regional and state awards. I like problems that span the whole stack, from PWM motor control up to production web apps real people use. Outside of engineering, I play chess, piano, and flute.
